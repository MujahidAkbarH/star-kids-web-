ABOUT
STAR KIDS GRAMMAR SCHOOL

Established in 2000 as a small Montessori setup for young learners, Star Kids Grammar Secondary School has grown into a well-recognized and trusted educational network in Korangi, Karachi. Over the years, Star Kids has become a symbol of quality and affordable education, offering classes from Montessori to Matriculation with a commitment to academic excellence and character building. With more than 25 years of educational excellence, Star Kids now operates two campuses and serves a student body of over 700 learners, making it one of the leading and most affordable schools in the area. A proud initiative of the Star Kids Schooling System, its sub-project, The Floret Academy, continues to expand creative and early learning opportunities for children. Registered with the Karachi Board since 2010, Star Kids maintains an impressive record in board examinations and has successfully guided many students toward reputable colleges and universities. Today, Star Kids stands as a strong, growing institution—recognized for its dedication, results, and over two decades of excellence in education.
