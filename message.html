Mr.Akber Hussain

I feel truly honored to be part of an institution that has upheld a strong tradition of dedicated educational service to the community. At Star Kids Grammar Secondary School, our foremost commitment is to provide quality education of high standards within our social and cultural framework, ensuring accessibility and affordability for all. Over the past 25 years, Star Kids has grown remarkably, now educating nearly 700 students across two campuses with the support of a team of passionate and devoted teachers. Our school offers well-equipped classrooms, computer and science laboratories, a well-stocked library, and modern learning aids, all designed to create an environment conducive to learning and personal growth. Since its establishment in 2000, Star Kids has continually advanced in academic excellence, producing students who go on to study in reputable colleges and universities across Pakistan. We firmly believe that every child deserves quality education, and with this belief, Star Kids continues to expand its reach through projects like The Floret Academy, ensuring meaningful and affordable education for every learner. We welcome all who share our vision to join us in our mission of nurturing young minds and shaping brighter futures."

  • Over the past 25 years, Star Kids Grammar Secondary School has grown into one of the most trusted and recognized educational institutions in Korangi, Karachi. Established in 2000 as a small Montessori setup, Star Kids has evolved into a well-established schooling system offering education from Montessori to Matriculation. Throughout its journey, the school has remained dedicated to bridging the gap between quality and affordability, ensuring that every child receives accessible, value-driven education. With its two campuses, experienced faculty, and strong academic record, Star Kids continues to play a vital role in shaping young minds and contributing to the advancement of education within the community.
  • Star Kids Grammar Secondary School strongly believes in and promotes women empowerment within its educational community. The school provides a female-friendly and inclusive environment, where a significant portion of the teaching and administrative staff are women. Under the guidance of Principal Miss Saira Tariq, Star Kids continues to encourage women to take on leadership and professional roles, reflecting the school’s commitment to equality, empowerment, and educational excellence.
  • With a vision to provide students with the tools, knowledge, and opportunities to build a brighter future, Star Kids Grammar Secondary School has continually expanded its educational initiatives. Through its sub-project, The Floret Academy, the school offers a creative and nurturing platform for early learners to explore and grow. Over the years, Star Kids has also collaborated with various local educational programs and community initiatives aimed at improving the quality and accessibility of education in Korangi and nearby areas. Guided by its mission of academic excellence and social responsibility, Star Kids remains dedicated to empowering students with the skills and confidence to become the leaders of tomorrow.
  • Mr. Akbar Hussain, the CEO of Star Kids Grammar Secondary School, is an experienced educationist with a strong background in school administration and academic leadership. He completed his higher education from the Swedish Institute of Educational Management, where he specialized in modern school leadership and curriculum development. With over 25 years of experience in the education sector, Mr. Hussain has played a vital role in transforming Star Kids into one of the most trusted and affordable schools in Korangi, Karachi. His vision focuses on academic excellence, teacher development, and accessible education for every child, ensuring that Star Kids continues to uphold its legacy of discipline, quality, and innovation in learning.
